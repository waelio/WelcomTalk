# Privacy Policy for WelcomTalk

**Last updated: April 1, 2026**

## Overview

WelcomTalk ("the app") does not collect, store, or transmit any personal data to any server or third party.

## Data Collection

WelcomTalk collects **no personal data**. The app does not:

- Create user accounts
- Transmit any data over the internet
- Connect to any server or backend service
- Track usage or analytics

## Peer-to-Peer Communication

All communication between devices occurs directly over your local network (Wi-Fi or Bluetooth) using Apple's Multipeer Connectivity framework. No data passes through any external server.

## Microphone & Speech Recognition

The microphone and speech recognition are used solely to transcribe your speaking turns locally on your device. Transcripts are stored only on your device and are never transmitted anywhere.

## Camera

The camera is used only to scan QR codes for joining a session. No images or video are stored or transmitted.

## Local Network

The app uses your local network solely to discover and connect directly with the other participant's device. No data is sent to the internet.

## Calendar

The app may add agreed meeting times to your device's local calendar. This data stays on your device.

## Contact
