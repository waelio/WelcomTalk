WelcomTalk helps two people have difficult conversations without interruptions, raised voices, or talking over each other.

One person speaks at a time. When your turn ends, the other person's turn begins — automatically. No buttons to fight over. No muting wars.

HOW IT WORKS
• Start a session and share a 6-character code with the other person via AirDrop, QR code, or NFC tap
• Both phones sync instantly — no internet required
• A visible countdown timer shows exactly how long each person has to speak
• When your turn is active your microphone is live; when it's not, you're muted
• Private notes let you jot thoughts while the other person speaks, without interrupting
• A full session log records every turn for review after the conversation ends
• Rate the conversation quality when you're done

GREAT FOR
• Couples working through disagreements
• Family discussions that tend to escalate
• Workplace conflict resolution
• Mediation and therapy sessions
• Any conversation that needs structure and fairness

FEATURES
• Turn-based audio with automatic mute enforcement
• Configurable turn duration and number of rounds
